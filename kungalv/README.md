# Case study — Kungälvs Kött & Chark

How [Kungälvs Kött & Chark](https://kungalvskottochchark.se) — local butcher in Kungälv — runs an online köttlåda store backed by a fast Astro frontend by [Webraketen](https://webraketen.se).

## Stack

- **Astro** for product pages + recipe content
- **Netlify** for hosting
- Order intake via form → email

## What Kungälvs Kött does

Köttlådor och styckdetaljer per kilo from 165 kr/kg. Lokal leverans i Kungälv, Göteborg och Västsverige. Fokus på svenskt ursprung, prisöppenhet och kunddialog.

→ [kungalvskottochchark.se](https://kungalvskottochchark.se)

## Recipe content

The site has a `/recept` section that scales — content marketing tied directly to product (which cut → which recipe → which köttlåda).
